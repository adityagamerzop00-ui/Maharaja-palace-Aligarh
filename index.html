About us · हमारे बारे में

A family hotel, steps from the station.

We are on Railway Road, Chauraha Mal Godown, opposite the city-side gate (platform 7) of Aligarh Junction.

Expect clean, comfortable rooms with marble floors, polite staff, home-style food, and a safe, quiet atmosphere. Ideal for families, exam visitors, business travellers and AMU visitors.
